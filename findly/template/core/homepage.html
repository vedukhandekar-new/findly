Features

Everything you need to recover items

Powerful tools built for owners, finders, and administrators

Report in 2 minutes

Fill a simple form with your item's description, category and photo. Our system starts scanning for matches immediately.

AI-Powered Matching

Our smart algorithm automatically matches lost and found items based on description, category and location.

Secure Chat

Private messaging system between owners and finders with identity protection until recovery is confirmed.

Instant Notifications

Real-time email and in-app alerts when a match is found, verified or when someone messages you.

Admin Control

Full admin dashboard to manage users, verify matches, block suspicious items and maintain platform safety.
